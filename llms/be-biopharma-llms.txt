> a therapeutic protein — for genetic disease, cancer and other serious conditions.

## Background

- Founded October 2020 by Longwood Fund with David Rawlings, M.D. and Richard James, Ph.D.
  of Seattle Children's Research Institute.
- Lead program BE-101: a functional Factor IX gene inserted into primary human B cells for
  hemophilia B; FDA Orphan Drug and Fast Track designations.
- Second program BE-102: hypophosphatasia.
- Financing: $82M (October 2024); $92M Series C (January 2025).
- First participant dosed in BeCoMe-9, July 2025.
